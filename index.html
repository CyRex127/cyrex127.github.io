About

I am an undergraduate student in the joint programme between Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and Queen Mary University of London, studying Telecommunications Engineering with Management.

My interests lie in multimodal learning, efficient inference, and generative models. I am exploring how model architectures and inference systems can work together to make visual understanding and generation more efficient.

Research interests

  1. 01 /

    Multimodal learning

    Vision–language models, video understanding, and the selection of informative visual data.

  2. 02 /

    Efficient inference

    Memory efficiency, caching, and inference systems for large multimodal models.

  3. 03 /

    Generative models

    Diffusion and flow models, transformer architectures, and efficient visual generation.
